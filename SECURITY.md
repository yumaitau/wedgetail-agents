# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/yumaitau/wedgetail-agents/security/advisories/new)
for this repository. Do not open a public issue.

Include the affected version, platform, and steps to reproduce. We aim to acknowledge reports
within three business days and to agree a disclosure date with you once a fix is ready.

## Supported versions

Security fixes go into the latest release. Upgrade with your OS package manager or the signed
packages on the releases page.

## Scope

The agent, its packaging (systemd unit, launchd plist, MSI, deb/rpm scripts) and the release
workflow. Issues in the Wedgetail service itself should be reported through your Wedgetail
support channel.
